COST-SENSITIVE LEARNING DEMO
============================================================

  Scenario: Fraud detection
  Cost of missed fraud (FN): $10,000
  Cost of false alarm  (FP): $50
  Cost ratio: 200:1
  Dataset: 10000 samples, 5.4% positive (fraud)

============================================================
PART 1: DEFAULT vs OPTIMAL THRESHOLD
============================================================

  Default threshold = 0.50:
    TP=91, FP=15, FN=70, TN=2824
    Accuracy: 0.972
    Missed frauds: 70  ×  $10,000 = $700,000
    False alarms:  15  ×  $50 = $750
    TOTAL COST: $700,750

  Optimal threshold = 0.01:
    TP=139, FP=659, FN=22, TN=2180
    Accuracy: 0.773
    Missed frauds: 22  ×  $10,000 = $220,000
    False alarms:  659  ×  $50 = $32,950
    TOTAL COST: $252,950

  Savings: $447,800 (64% reduction)

============================================================
PART 2: DIFFERENT COST RATIOS → DIFFERENT THRESHOLDS
============================================================

  Scenario              Cost FN  Cost FP  Best t   Total cost
  ---------------------------------------------------------
  Equal costs          $    100 $    100    0.35 $      8,200
  FN 10x more          $  1,000 $    100    0.08 $     51,700
  FN 200x more         $ 10,000 $     50    0.01 $    252,950
  FP 5x more           $    100 $    500    0.92 $     11,800

  → When FN is expensive, threshold drops (catch more positives)
  → When FP is expensive, threshold rises (fewer false alarms)

============================================================
PART 3: EXPECTED VALUE PER PREDICTION
============================================================

  For each prediction, compute:
    E[cost if flag]    = P(legit) × cost_FP = (1-p) × $50
    E[cost if ignore]  = P(fraud) × cost_FN = p × $10000
    Flag if E[cost if ignore] > E[cost if flag]
    i.e., p × 10000 > (1-p) × 50
    i.e., p > 50 / (10000 + 50) = 0.0050

  Theoretical optimal threshold: 0.0050
  Empirical optimal threshold:   0.01
  (They differ because the model's probabilities aren't perfectly calibrated)

  Sample decisions:
    Prob | E[cost ignore] | E[cost flag] | Decision
  ----------------------------------------------------
    0.01 | $          100 | $         50 | FLAG
    0.05 | $          500 | $         48 | FLAG
    0.10 | $        1,000 | $         45 | FLAG
    0.30 | $        3,000 | $         35 | FLAG
    0.50 | $        5,000 | $         25 | FLAG
    0.80 | $        8,000 | $         10 | FLAG
