============================================================
WHY SOFT LABELS HELP
============================================================

Example (digit 8):
  Hard label: [8]  → 'it's definitely a 8'
  Soft label: [0.014 0.006 0.01  0.02  0.004 0.008 0.024 0.002 0.892 0.02 ]
  Top-3 classes: 8=0.892, 6=0.024, 3=0.020
  → Soft labels say '8 looks a bit like 6 and 3'
  This similarity structure helps the student generalize better!
