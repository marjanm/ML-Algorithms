============================================================
   Key LLM Concepts Demonstrated:
============================================================
  - Autoregressive generation: predict one token at a time
  - Temperature: controls randomness (0 = deterministic, >1 = creative)
  - Top-k sampling: only consider the k most likely next tokens
  - Top-p (nucleus): sample from smallest set of tokens summing to p
  - Repetition penalty: discourage repeating the same phrases
  - The model was never trained on these specific prompts
    — it generalises from patterns seen in its training data
