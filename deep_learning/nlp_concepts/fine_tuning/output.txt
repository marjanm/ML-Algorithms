======================================================================
  FINE-TUNING vs TRANSFER LEARNING  —  Demo
======================================================================
  Base model: distilbert-base-uncased
  Task: binary sentiment classification
  Train: 20 examples,  Test: 8 examples

  1. Zero-shot (pre-trained SST-2 model, no fine-tuning):
     ✓ "An outstanding and moving film               " → POSITIVE (0.999)
     ✓ "I really enjoyed this movie                  " → POSITIVE (0.999)
     ✓ "Absolutely dreadful experience               " → NEGATIVE (0.995)
     ✓ "Would not recommend to anyone                " → NEGATIVE (0.980)
     ✓ "Beautiful cinematography and great script    " → POSITIVE (0.999)
     ✗ "The worst film of the year                   " → POSITIVE (0.999)
     ✓ "A truly magical experience                   " → POSITIVE (0.998)
     ✓ "So bad I walked out halfway                  " → NEGATIVE (0.999)
     Accuracy: 87.50%

  2. Fine-tuning DistilBERT (3 epochs on our tiny dataset):
     Epoch 1: loss = 0.6916
     Epoch 2: loss = 0.6074
     Epoch 3: loss = 0.4548
     Training time: 1.0s
     Test results:
     ✓ "An outstanding and moving film               " → POSITIVE (0.643)
     ✓ "I really enjoyed this movie                  " → POSITIVE (0.674)
     ✓ "Absolutely dreadful experience               " → NEGATIVE (0.704)
     ✓ "Would not recommend to anyone                " → NEGATIVE (0.635)
     ✓ "Beautiful cinematography and great script    " → POSITIVE (0.716)
     ✓ "The worst film of the year                   " → NEGATIVE (0.789)
     ✓ "A truly magical experience                   " → POSITIVE (0.614)
     ✓ "So bad I walked out halfway                  " → NEGATIVE (0.751)
     Accuracy: 100.00%

  Summary:
    Zero-shot (SST-2 pre-trained) : 87.50%
    Fine-tuned (3 epochs, 20 ex.) : 100.00%

  Key concepts:
    - Transfer learning: use a model trained on task A for task B (no retraining)
    - Fine-tuning: retrain the model's weights on your specific data
    - Even 20 examples + 3 epochs can adapt a pre-trained model
    - Pre-trained models already understand language — you just teach them your task
